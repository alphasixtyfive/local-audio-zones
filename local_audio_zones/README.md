# Local Audio Zones

Use your soundcard outputs as separate Music Assistant rooms. Choose a card
and output pair for each room in the app settings. Music Assistant handles
playback, volume and groups.

Optional USB relays turn an amplifier on while its rooms are playing and off
after they stop. No Home Assistant automation is needed for direct USB control.

Open the **Documentation** tab for setup, output selection and amplifier triggers.
The app is experimental while testing across more hardware continues.
